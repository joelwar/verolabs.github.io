Illustrative engagements

How transformation becomes practical.

The work is always tailored to the operation. These anonymized examples show the kinds of problems I help leaders solve—and the practical, human-centered systems that follow.

01

Accelerating a high-volume monthly review

Click to reveal the engagement

A business operations team once spent days combing through hundreds or thousands of monthly records by hand. Together, we designed an AI-driven, human-in-the-loop workflow that was validated against known records before launch. Review-ready spreadsheets surfaced likely exceptions with clear highlighting; people made the final calls, focusing expert attention where it mattered and turning a multi-person, multi-day effort into a dramatically faster control process.

02

Making service delivery performance actionable

Click to reveal the engagement

For a service organization, we brought together the signals behind everyday delivery—workload, timeliness, quality, and other relevant factors—into a properly validated, AI-assisted operational view. Practical dashboards gave leaders a better way to coach teams, recognize progress, and improve throughput. Human judgment stayed at the center: managers could investigate context and make the decisions that affected their teams.

Examples are intentionally anonymized and representative. No client names, departments, or confidential performance figures are disclosed.
